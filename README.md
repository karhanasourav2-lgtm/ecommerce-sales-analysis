🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, product trends, and regional performance.

The goal is to turn raw sales data into useful business insights that can support better decisions.

🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Compare sales across regions.
- Understand changes in business performance.
- Support data-driven decision-making.

🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Sales by Product
- Sales by Region

KPIs will be calculated from the project dataset.

🔍 Analysis Performed

- Reviewed sales and profit data.
- Explored product and regional performance.
- Identified useful trends and performance differences.
- Prepared data for reporting and visualization.

💡 Business Value

This analysis can help a business understand which products and regions perform well and where profitability may need improvement.
